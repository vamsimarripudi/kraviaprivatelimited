# Professional review

The system supports private review records with statuses `READY_FOR_REVIEW`, `IN_REVIEW`, `CHANGES_REQUIRED`, `APPROVED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE` and `REVIEW_PENDING`.

## Required review packages

- Company Secretary: company facts, governance workflows, registers, notices, minutes, resolutions and disclosures.
- Chartered Accountant: GST, finance evidence, bank/reconciliation workflows and tax evidence.
- Legal counsel: public claims, Privacy/DPDP, Terms, security language, careers privacy and contracts.
- Directors: public facts, launch approval, trust claims, disclosures and privileged production access.

Codex may mark a package ready for review only. Professional approval must name the real reviewer, date, scope and approved version.
