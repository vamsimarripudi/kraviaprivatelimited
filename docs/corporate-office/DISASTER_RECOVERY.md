# Disaster recovery

Before applying to production, verify the selected Supabase plan's backup scope, retention, point-in-time recovery and restore procedure. Record the responsible owner and complete a restore test. Do not claim that records are backed up until this is configured and tested.

Maintain an approved emergency-access and continuity process for Supabase/Auth outage, accidental deletion, corrupted record and unavailable website. Production data must not be copied to development without an approved and protected process.
