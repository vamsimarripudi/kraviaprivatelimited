# Review governance

Content is assigned by domain: content, product, technology, security, privacy, legal, corporate or director. High-risk wording (`100%`, `fully compliant`, `certified`, `guaranteed`, and similar) is flagged for review; it is not silently approved or mechanically deleted.

Policies and corporate disclosures require legal and corporate review. Security/privacy content must be mapped to its appropriate specialist review before real publication. No ordinary editor can bypass those controls.
