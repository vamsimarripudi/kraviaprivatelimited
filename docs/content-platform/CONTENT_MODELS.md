# Content models

Supported public content domains: Company, Product, Milestone, Principle, Leadership, Technology, Research, News, Press Release, Engineering Article, Trust Document, Policy, Corporate Disclosure, Report, Career, Partner and FAQ.

All records carry a stable slug, status, visibility, ownership, review dates, version and SEO configuration. Corporate facts have effective dates and evidence references. Metrics, testimonials, partner and customer records are intentionally not seeded; their public use needs evidence and approval.
