# Newsroom

`/newsroom` is an editorial landing page for approved Company, Product, Engineering, Research and Press content. Every published item has a stable slug, canonical, publication date, summary, author, RSS entry and Article/NewsArticle schema where applicable.

Press releases are version-locked: material corrections require a correction note and a revised record, never a silent rewrite. With no approved updates, the public Newsroom deliberately shows an honest empty state.
