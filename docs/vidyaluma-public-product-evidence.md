# VidyaLuma public product evidence register

Reviewed: 2026-11-10 (IST)
Purpose: keep KRAVIA's public VidyaLuma descriptions grounded in the first-party product website, without disclosing student or school records.

## Public sources
- [Product overview](https://vidyaluma.in/product): school operations, attendance, fees, documents, academic records, teacher review, and role-aware access.
- [Solutions](https://vidyaluma.in/solutions): school leadership, classrooms, operations and school-approved family information.
- [Pricing](https://vidyaluma.in/pricing): live catalogue of school plans, usage limits, and a guided sales/Launchpad onboarding process. Prices can change; KRAVIA does not hardcode the catalogue.
- [FAQ](https://vidyaluma.in/faq): attendance exceptions; teacher-scanned corrected pages; AI observations subject to educator approval; approved parent report links; school-controlled access and user responsibilities.
- [Trust](https://vidyaluma.in/trust): published student and school data protection, AI, access and sharing, retention, and security commitments.
- [Official product homepage](https://vidyaluma.in/): the public product brand remains VidyaLuma; KRAVIA PRIVATE LIMITED is the developing corporate entity.

## Publication rules
- Distinguish what the published documentation describes from which workflows are enabled for a particular school and release.
- Never claim verified adoption, revenue, partners, funding, school counts, exam outcomes, certifications, or universal availability without appropriate evidence.
- AI supports educator workflows and does not independently authorise student reports.
- Parents receive school-authorised information only, not unrestricted internal school accounts.
- Public demonstrations use fictional student data and no production records.
- Preserve the existing official VidyaLuma logo and the KRAVIA brand assets; the content enhancement does not authorise any visual redesign.
- Treat the active VidyaLuma pricing catalogue as authoritative. Do not copy prices into a static corporate overview.

## Corporate presentation targets
- Homepage portfolio entry: concrete flagship product purpose, brief.
- `/products/vidyaluma`: full product story, role-aware workflows, approval boundaries, live public evidence links, FAQs.
- `/company/startup-profile`: proprietary ownership, product model, independently inspectable public URLs.
- SEO/structured data: no inflated feature, performance, or commercial claims.
