# KRAVIA Office V1 completion scope

V1 completion means the governed operating foundation is implemented and passing repository quality gates: identity/AAL2, permission and device controls, workforce directory/presence/attendance, requests/approvals, Company Inbox tasks, notifications, company calendar, CRM pipeline, engineering control center, existing finance/governance/document/customer/product modules, and a read-first intelligence boundary. Live third-party actions remain dependent on explicit provider connection and authorization; no credential or statutory state is fabricated.
