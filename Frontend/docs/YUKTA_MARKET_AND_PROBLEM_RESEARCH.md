## Category and selection

Practice-management systems are the closest accessible researched category for the documented outpatient focus. Publisher definitions include software and services broader than V1. Hospital information systems, EHR, revenue-cycle management and digital health overlap but are not additive. Total healthcare spending is not software revenue. ## Problem evidence and mechanisms

[Ranjan et al., Cureus, 5 April 2025](https://assets.cureus.com/uploads/original_article/pdf/351844/20250405-27095-aj5o4n.pdf) examines online versus conventional OPD registration at S.N. Medical College, Agra. The small single-centre sample (40 patients, 20 per route) and observational comparison are not national benchmarks or causal YUKTA evidence. PDF text was inspected; visual PDF rendering was unavailable. Numerical improvement results are deliberately excluded. The MoHFW registration programme provides national infrastructure context, not willingness-to-pay evidence.

| Persona / friction | Evidence basis | Designed mechanism | Future measure | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reception: registration steps and waiting | Agra study; foundation workflows | Returning-patient context, appointments and arrival | Registration start → valid visit | Single-centre external evidence, no product outcome |
| Front desk: schedule changes | Product workflow design | Doctor timeline and queue state | Median/P90 arrival-to-consultation | Hypothesis requiring pilot baseline |
| Doctor/assistant: report handoff | V1 authority boundary | Separate report-ready and doctor-reviewed | Ready-to-reviewed elapsed time | Not evidence of clinical benefit |
| Accounts: payment verification | V1 operations design | Separate bill, collection and settlement states | Unresolved reconciliation age | No live payment integration asserted |
| Patient/staff: follow-up | V1 scope | Doctor-defined follow-up coordination | Eligible follow-ups completed | No adherence or health-outcome promise |
