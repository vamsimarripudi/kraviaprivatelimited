# KRAVIA 50-Company Corporate Website Benchmark

Reviewed: 14 August 2026
Method: Official public homepages plus their visible navigation, footer, and public resource paths were sampled. This is a structural benchmark, not a claim that every company exposes every capability. Limited crawl access is recorded rather than guessed at.

## Cross-company pattern matrix

| Pattern visible in official public footprint | Observed | Share | Kravia decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Dedicated company/about or equivalent story | 45 | 90% | Adopt: Company is a first-class domain. |

Counts reflect the official public footprint observed on the review date, not private portals or inferred operations.

## Patterns applied to Kravia

- Homepage expanded into identity, company, purpose, portfolio, operating method, principles, technology/governance, corporate information, pre-footer and corporate footer.
- New public-ready routes: company/about, company/principles, company/corporate-information, technology/engineering and technology/ai.
- Governed content models for public company facts, products and future milestones. Unverified identifiers remain absent.
- A multi-column footer with a readable corporate identity layer.
- SEO/AEO/GEO implementation based on crawlable HTML, unique route metadata, canonical URLs, a safe sitemap, preview noindex behavior and truthful structured data.
- Future newsroom, research, partners, investor and developer architecture remains unpublished until real approved content exists.
