# Compliance engine

Rules are stored as a rule, version, effective period, applicability expression, calculation definition, official source and professional-review status. Old legal rule versions are preserved. Company applicability facts are separately sourced and reviewed.

Obligations record the calculated date, override, explanation, owner, evidence and state. The interface must describe a recommendation, source and review status; it must not declare legal compliance. Overrides preserve original calculated value, reason, actor and source reference.

Do not seed statutory deadlines from memory. Sources must be official and current, then reviewed by Kravia's CA/CS/legal adviser.
