# Operating guide

1. Create a meeting as PROPOSED, assign attendees, agenda and supporting records.
2. Obtain the required internal/professional review, then issue a notice and capture delivery evidence.
3. Record attendance and draft minutes in a new version; route comments and review without editing an approved version.
4. An authorised AAL2 actor finalises approved minutes. Corrections are linked amendments, never silent edits.
5. Create obligations only from reviewed rules, attach evidence before filing/completion and retain any professional override.
6. Upload documents into the private vault with metadata/classification. Assign professionals narrowly and set expiry.
7. Publish a public disclosure only through its separate review/approval workflow; revoke public access through unpublish, preserving history.
8. Offboard users by revoking roles/sessions/assignments; retain their corporate records and audit history.
