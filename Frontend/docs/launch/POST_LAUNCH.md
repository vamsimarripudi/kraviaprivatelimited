# Post-launch operations

For the initial launch period, monitor public errors, form delivery, Auth failures, Storage denials, automation failures, 404 volume and email routing.

After launch:

- Verify sitemap and canonical URLs before submitting to search engines.
- Record performance baseline for primary public routes.
- Review access, professional feedback, data-migration warnings and content corrections.
- Schedule freshness reviews for company facts, legal pages, DPDP links, Trust content, disclosures and products.
- Route production fixes through CI and controlled migrations; do not use uncontrolled hotfixes.
