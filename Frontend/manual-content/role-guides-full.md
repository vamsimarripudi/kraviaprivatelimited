### Purpose of this role

Company-wide governance, delegated access, strategy, selected technical oversight and lawful authority decisions; retain separation between oversight and execution.

### Actual access identity

`OWNER` is a protected technical role. Do not treat Founder authority as permission to circumvent Finance, statutory appointment, department handover or independent approvals.

### Routes and where to go

### First-day / first-access checklist

1. Open `/office/dashboard` to review assigned work and exception summaries.
2. Confirm your identity, approved device and current scoped rights at `/office/settings`.
3. Review `/office/role-packages` and `/office/access` to understand active staff and delegated authority
4. do not create synthetic records.
5. Open `/office/approvals` to identify pending decisions requiring an independent reviewer.

### Daily / recurring operating sequence

1. Review decisions and risks in `/office/decisions`, goals in `/office/strategy`, and project exceptions in `/office/portfolio`.
2. Use `/office/access` to approve legitimate roles, device/MFA requests, and access reviews with a reason.
3. Confirm ownership proposals in `/office/department-ownership` do not take effect until the recipient accepts.
4. Review audit evidence before closing a sensitive transaction
5. ensure the receiving department retains authority after transfer.

### Worked example — from start to completion

When a department lead needs ongoing authority: verify their identity and role; review current ownership; propose the appropriate transfer in `/office/department-ownership`; have the recipient independently accept in their own MFA session; then check audit and read/write restrictions. Do not accept on their behalf.

### What counts as completed

Owner-approved business decision with recorded reason, proper checker, current department ownership and an audit trail, not a silent permission override.

### Safety and authority boundary

No employee approval of their own compensation, no fabricated signatures, no payment from a draft, no second Owner by routine admin actions. Founder view can be read-only after ownership transfer.

### When blocked, denied or uncertain

For blocked executive decisions use `/office/requests`; for IT/security incidents use `/office/it` where assigned and escalate to the current accountable professional. Always record the actual error, time and affected section without sharing passwords, codes, raw bank details or identity documents. A missing sidebar link may reflect a deliberate permission rule.

### End of day, transfer or employment exit

Review outstanding assigned work in your task/request queues. Hand off context and supporting records to an authorized recipient, complete actual attendance checkout if applicable, and **Sign out**. For offboarding, follow an approved HR/Access workflow; do not simply delete local files or assume a user was deactivated.
